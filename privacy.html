CURRENCY LAB / PRIVACY

Privacy, clearly.

Currency Lab is designed to work without an account, advertising, or tracking. This policy explains what stays on your device and what happens when the app requests exchange rates.

Effective September 18, 2026
Data collection Local preferences Exchange-rate service Contact

Data collection

Currency Lab does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking and does not require an account.

Local preferences

Your selected base currency and favorite currencies are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. These preferences are not sent to the developer.

Exchange-rate service

To provide exchange rates and historical series, the app sends currency codes and requested date ranges to the public Frankfurter API. These requests do not include your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers. The external service may process standard network information required to answer an internet request.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact rzazadeh@icloud.com.
