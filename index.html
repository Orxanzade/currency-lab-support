Support

Currency Lab helps you convert currencies, compare the latest available exchange rates, and review historical movements with clear charts.

Using the converter

Tap the amount to edit it or use the calculator. Tap a currency code to choose another currency. Use the refresh button to request the latest available rates.

Rates and charts

Rates and historical series are supplied by the Frankfurter API. Values are informational and may differ from rates offered by banks or payment providers.
